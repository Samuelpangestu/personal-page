# Claude for Startups — Draft Application Answers (QAPilot)

Draft answers for the Anthropic Claude for Startups application form,
rewritten around the product pivot to **QAPilot** — an AI assistant for
QA/engineering teams (API test generation, Gherkin scenario writing, CI
failure triage), built on Claude.

Honesty note: the company status is deliberately stated as-is (not yet
incorporated, idea stage) rather than fabricated — the application brief
identified fabricating legal facts as a likely reason the earlier
application was rejected.

---

**Company founding date**
> QAPilot is not yet a legally incorporated company — it is currently at
> the idea and pre-incorporation stage, built and iterated on by a single
> founder. Formal registration (most likely as an Indonesian PT
> Perorangan, the simplified single-founder entity available through the
> OSS system) is planned once the product has gone through an initial
> round of validation with real QA and engineering users, rather than
> registering a legal entity ahead of having anything to validate.

**Funding round / investors**
> QAPilot is fully bootstrapped and has not raised any external funding,
> and there are currently no investors involved. All development so far
> — the product concept, the website, and early prototyping work — has
> been self-funded by the founder. The plan is to keep validating real
> demand from QA and engineering teams first, and only pursue outside
> funding once there is clearer evidence the product solves a problem
> people will actually pay for.

**How are you building this**
> QAPilot is being built solo by a single founder with a direct
> background in QA and test automation (hands-on experience with
> Appium, Cucumber, K6, and Postman), using Claude Code as the primary
> tool across the entire development cycle — from thinking through
> architecture and product structure, to writing and reviewing code, to
> quickly iterating based on feedback. Working this way lets one person
> move at a pace that would normally require a small team, while staying
> closely hands-on with every part of the product rather than
> delegating core decisions away.

**What support do you need from Anthropic**
> The most valuable support would be API access and credits to allow
> for broader experimentation as the product moves from idea to working
> prototype, since usage-based testing is central to validating whether
> Claude can reliably generate accurate API tests and Gherkin scenarios.
> Beyond that, guidance on best practices for building agentic,
> Claude-based tools specifically for software testing workflows would
> help avoid early missteps, and exposure to the broader community of
> startups building on Claude would be valuable for learning from others
> further along a similar path.

**% of AI spend on Anthropic**
> Close to 100% of AI spend goes to Anthropic — Claude is the only AI
> model used anywhere in the current workflow, both for building the
> QAPilot product itself and for day-to-day development work more
> broadly, including the website this application links to. There is no
> meaningful usage of any other AI provider at this stage.

**What are you building on Claude**
> QAPilot is an AI assistant for QA and engineering teams that uses
> Claude as its reasoning engine to draft API tests, write Gherkin
> scenarios for BDD workflows, and summarize likely causes of CI test
> failures — all generated from context that already exists in a team's
> repository, such as endpoint definitions, specs, or user stories.
> The goal is not to replace human QA judgment but to remove the
> repetitive, time-consuming first draft of test-writing work, so QA
> engineers can spend their time reviewing, refining, and deciding what
> actually ships, rather than starting every test from a blank page.
